Mesh Volumes

A separate path-traced gallery inspired by Alex Tardif's participating-media
examples. Five exhibits compare three bunny media, a hollow teapot and a blue
trefoil knot. The meshes provide density shapes; they are not opaque render
surfaces. Room surfaces and shadows use the normal path-tracing geometry path.

Open Mesh Volumes in the world selector. The Scene and Game cameras share the
opening view. Navigate in Scene with RMB and WASD; keep the camera stationary
to accumulate samples. No scripts or external project assets are required.

Select a volume entity and open its volume material to change absorption,
scattering, density or phase anisotropy. In Cloud authoring, the Closed mesh
layer points to a bundled OBJ. Bake cloudscape rebuilds the shape; save the
material to retain the result. Source geometry is uniformly fitted into the
volume box with a 5 percent margin. Wind is disabled so accumulation converges.

Bunny and teapot grids are 160 cubed; the knot is 128 cubed. Bunny variants use
the same shape with different optical properties. The original teapot has
hollow walls: its component density multiplier is deliberately higher (12).
All sources are closed manifolds, and saved grids were round-trip checked with
the production material decoder. Baked data uses the engine's lossless compact
voxel encoding; it needs no first-open bake or download. This project adds no
renderer code and does not change Clouds and Fog.

Asset provenance
Bunny: simplified geometry from Mikoto's existing Prefabs/bunny/bunny.obj,
Stanford Bunny, Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory. 32,000 triangles;
closed topology checked after simplification. Stanford's distribution terms
allow research/free redistribution with acknowledgement, and require permission
for commercial use. Keep this attribution with redistributions:
https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/
Teapot: geometry-only copy of Mikoto's existing Prefabs/teapot/teapot.obj
(the Utah teapot model). No new licence is asserted for that bundled source.
Trefoil knot and gallery primitives: procedurally generated for Mikoto.
Rajdhani Regular: bundled with its SIL Open Font License in Assets/Fonts/OFL.txt.

The centre bunny is an absorption-dominant smoke study. A bundled 64-cubed
warped density grid intersects its closed mesh in the saved authoring recipe.
Rebaking retains the breakup. This is a stationary smoke snapshot so path
tracing can accumulate; density 4, absorption 0.5/m, scattering 0.3/m, g 0.25.
