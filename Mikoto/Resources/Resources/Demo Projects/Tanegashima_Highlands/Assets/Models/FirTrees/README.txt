Fir-tree demo import

Original asset and author attribution are in license.txt (CC BY 4.0).
Nine tree variants use the supplied LOD1 and LOD2 geometry. Positions, normals
and tangents are baked upright and centered at the base for instancing.
The demo uses alpha-tested needles (cutoff 0.45), rather than blended cards.
Middle LODs are placed by distance from the initial lookout; this is authored
placement, not an automatic LOD system. Original UVs and textures are retained.
