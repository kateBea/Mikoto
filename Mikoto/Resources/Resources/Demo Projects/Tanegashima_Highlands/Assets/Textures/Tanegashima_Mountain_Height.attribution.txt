Derived from Mapzen Terrain Tiles / Tilezen Joerd elevation products.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/

Global SRTM and GMTED2010 terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
Global ETOPO1 terrain data: DOC/NOAA/NESDIS/NCEI, U.S. Department of Commerce.
These credits apply to the
global elevation products used here. US government source datasets are
public-domain data; this provenance notice is retained with the derivative.
No Google satellite imagery is redistributed in this project.

Changes: bilinear DEM resampling into a metre-based local grid; horizontal
scale 0.2876; vertical scale 0.4314; removal of neighbouring disconnected
land; synthetic bathymetry; a small authored inland lake. The result is not
survey-grade geographic data. Height encoding: unsigned 16-bit greyscale,
world elevation = -80 + (sample / 65535) * 280 metres.
